# Thumb Slice Rush

A mobile-first, full-screen arcade fruit slicer. Fruit bursts out from a glowing center portal; tap or swipe to launch unlimited knives toward it. Slice fruit to score points.

## Play
Open `index.html` directly, or serve this folder with a static server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Controls
- Tap toward a fruit to launch a knife from the center portal.
- Swipe to aim a knife in the direction of the swipe.
- Every sliced fruit is worth 10 points; best score is saved in your browser.
- Use the music-note button to toggle sound effects.
